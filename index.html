Questions

Before you order

Do all products include a certificate of analysis?

Yes. Every vial ships with the COA for its exact lot, including the HPLC chromatogram and mass spectrometry result. Digital copies are sent with the tracking email.

How should material be stored on arrival?

Lyophilized powder is stable at 2–8 °C and should be kept sealed, dry and away from light. Long-term storage is best at −20 °C. A handling sheet is included in every shipment.

Which shipping and payment methods are available?

Tracked priority shipping across the 50 states, with same-day dispatch on orders confirmed before 2 PM ET. Payment by bank transfer, card or crypto; institutional purchase orders are accepted on request.

Can you supply bulk quantities or custom blends?

Yes. Send the compound, quantity and target purity and we will return a quote with lead time, typically within one business day.

Who can buy from PEP-T+ Labs?

Sales are limited to qualified research professionals and institutions, for laboratory research use only. We do not provide dosing guidance and our products are not intended for human or animal use.
